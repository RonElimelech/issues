### Goals
- The trainee will understand the concepts relating to day-to-day maintenance of a service, including the impotrance of being on-call, effective alerting, effective troubleshooting and incident management.

> Some concepts discussed in this subchapter may feel a bit distant but we believe that in-time that will make sense as you grow as a member in an IDFcTS team. Take special note of being on-call and its crucial importance in an organization such as IDFcTS.

### Tasks
- Read https://sre.google/sre-book/practical-alerting/ and answer the following questions: 
  - What are some characteristics of a “good alert”? Why are these qualities important?
  - Explain how alerting on “black box” metrics differs from “white box” metrics. Provide examples of each.
  - Your team is consistently alerted for high CPU usage on a service, but this rarely indicates a real issue. Describe the process you would use to refine or remove this alert.
- Read https://sre.google/sre-book/being-on-call/ and answer the following questions:
  - What are the primary responsibilities of an SRE while on-call?
  - What is the “follow the sun” model for on-call, and what are its benefits?
  - During your on-call shift, you receive an alert for an issue that has self-resolved in the past. How would you verify the current status and decide on an appropriate response?
- Read https://sre.google/sre-book/effective-troubleshooting/ and answer the following questions:
  - Define "what", "where", "why" approach to troubleshooting.
  - What are the key steps in a systematic troubleshooting process?
  - Imagine you are faced with a service that is responding slowly. Describe the steps you would take to diagnose the issue systematically.
  - Why is it critical to maintain a balance between speed and thoroughness in the troubleshooting process?
- Read https://sre.google/sre-book/emergency-response/ and answer the following questions:
  - What are the key components of an effective incident response plan?
- Read https://sre.google/sre-book/managing-incidents/ and answer the following questions:
  - How can you balance the need for rapid incident resolution with the importance of thorough root cause analysis?
  - You are tasked with improving your team's emergency response capabilities. What initiatives would you propose to enhance readiness?
- Read https://sre.google/sre-book/postmortem-culture/ and answer the following questions:
  - What is a postmortem? Why is it important?
  - After a postmortem, what actions would you take to ensure that identified improvements are implemented effectively?
  - You are tasked with facilitating a postmortem after a major outage. Describe the steps you would take to ensure a productive discussion.
- Bonus: As discussed in this subchapter and the previous one, monitoring is an essential skill for any SRE. Learn about Prometheus and Grafana, set up an httpd application, and monitor its status using the blackbox_exporter from https://github.com/prometheus/blackbox_exporter. Create an alert for a scenario of your choice, trigger it, and display the alert in Grafana.
