### Goals
- The trainee will familiarize themselves with concepts such as SLIs, SLOs, Toil, Monitoring and Automation.
- The trainee will understand the importance of system observability and know to distinguish between different types of signals.

### Tasks
- Read https://sre.google/sre-book/embracing-risk/ and answer the following questions:
  - What defines "service reliability"? How can you track it?
- Read https://sre.google/sre-book/service-level-objectives/ and answer the following questions:
  - What is a Service Level Objective (SLO), and how does it differ from a Service Level Indicator (SLI)?
  - Define an error budget. How does it relate to SLOs?
  - What role does user behavior play in determining appropriate SLOs? Why is understanding user expectations crucial?
  - Discuss why overachieving on reliability (i.e., consistently exceeding the SLOs) can actually be a drawback. What are the trade-offs?
- Read https://sre.google/sre-book/eliminating-toil/ and answer the following questions:
  - Define “toil” in the context of Site Reliability Engineering. What characteristics make a task qualify as toil?
  - Discuss the potential risks of not addressing toil within an SRE team. How might this impact team morale, productivity, and the overall stability of services?
  - In your opinion, should reducing toil be an organization-wide effort or solely the responsibility of an individual team? Support your argument.
- Read https://sre.google/sre-book/monitoring-distributed-systems/ and answer the following questions:
  - What are the Four Golden Signals in distributed system monitoring, and why are they essential? Give an example for each.
  - Describe the difference between black-box and white-box monitoring. When might each approach be preferable?
  - How do SLIs relate to monitoring?
  - Explain why “alert fatigue” can be problematic in an SRE team. How does effective monitoring help to prevent it?
- Read https://sre.google/sre-book/automation-at-google/ and answer the following questions:
  - Describe the relationship between automation and reliability in distributed systems. How does automation support system stability?
  - What role does error budget play in prioritizing automation projects in SRE?
  - Discuss the potential downsides of “over-automation” and how it might affect system reliability or team productivity.
  - Your team manually provisions resources when demand spikes, which takes time and creates inconsistencies. Describe how you’d design an automation solution to dynamically manage resources based on demand.
- Read https://sre.google/sre-book/simplicity/ and answer the following questions:
  - Define complexity in the context of SRE. How does it impact system reliability and maintainability?
  - What are some signs that a system has become too complex to manage effectively?
  - Explain the risks and potential benefits of re-architecting a system in order to achieve simplicity. What factors should influence whether such a re-architecture is worth the investment?
  - Bonus: Read https://how.complexsystems.fail/
