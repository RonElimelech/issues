### Goals
- The trainee will understand the meaning of Site Reliability Engineering and how it is related to IDFcTS

> In this chapter, we’ll be referencing the Google SRE Book (commonly known simply as the "SRE Book"), which established the Site Reliability Engineering (SRE) framework. This book covers many day-to-day practices that often become second nature to experienced IDFcTS team members and, as a result, aren’t discussed as much as other, more technical topics. While this chapter is slightly more theoretical than previous ones, the importance of the material you’re about to read cannot be overstated. Beyond reading, it’s essential to fully understand, question, and reflect on the topics presented here, as they are at the core of what we do in IDFcTS.
