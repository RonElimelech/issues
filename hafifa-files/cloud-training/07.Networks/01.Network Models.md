### Goals
- The trainee will understand why network models were created, the limitation of the OSI model and the concept of encapsulation.

### Tasks
- Learn about the OSI models, focus on the following:
    - Why the OSI model was created? 
    - What does it mean that the OSI model is layered? Why was it designed this way?
    - The OSI model breaks a flow of communication into 7 abstraction layers. Explain each layer and give an example of at least 1 protocol per layer.

- What is the TCP/IP protocol suite? 

- Describe what encapsulation is, give a practical example of encapsulation. Whay are the benefits of delivering a packet through a network by encapsulating it on different steps of the traffic flow.
